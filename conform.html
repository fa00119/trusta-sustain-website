Current evidence boundary

CONFORM currently has a research and controlled demonstration workflow; an independently deployed public client-facing demo and third-party accreditation have not been verified. Its labels are internal decision-support states, not regulatory certification, settlement assurance or independent professional audit.

Design-partner validation

Trusta seeks a narrowly scoped, preferably paid design-partner pilot with a named decision owner, baseline, evidence permissions, measurable acceptance criteria, and agreed reporting. University or independent lab participation can be arranged when it provides useful validation; no partnership is implied until confirmed.

Start with non-confidential information only. Pricing and any handling of private operating data require a written proposal, signed agreement and approved secure intake. No meeting or purchase is created by clicking the email link.
