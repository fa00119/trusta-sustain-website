Commercial wedge

ORBIT Large-Load Site Constraint Screen

A bounded cross-utility diagnostic for data-center, AI-compute and industrial projects adding roughly 5-100 MW of load, where a late-discovered utility or permit constraint can strand schedule or capital.

The decision question

Before committing more land, design effort or capital: identify the most likely binding constraint across power, cooling, water and wastewater; estimate when it becomes binding; compare realistic sequencing or mitigation options; and state what evidence is still required before implementation.

  • Site / parcel and proposed load phasing
  • Known electrical service, queue or interconnection context
  • Cooling architecture and major thermal assumptions
  • Water source, demand basis and known allocation limits
  • Wastewater / pretreatment limits and discharge assumptions
  • Brownfield utility history where available

Decision package

1. Constraint map and headroom table

2. Evidence register and assumption log

3. Base / alternative scenario comparison

4. CAPEX / OPEX and timing implications where supportable

5. What would change the conclusion

6. Ranked next actions and validation plan

Source-linked inputs, customer-stated inputs, modeled assumptions, gaps and the specific evidence that would change or strengthen the recommendation.

Finding

Peak cooling distribution—not nominal central-plant tonnage—is the modeled limiting condition for the next load increment under the supplied scenario.

ORBIT answer contract

Seven questions every material recommendation must answer.

Constraint + significance

What is binding or valuable, what evidence supports it, and why does it matter operationally?

Action + alternatives

What should be done, what realistic alternatives exist, and what happens under each scenario?

Impact + confidence

What are the operational/economic consequences, how confident are they, and what evidence should be collected next?
