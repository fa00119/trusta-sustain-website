Primary commercial product

ORBIT Large-Load Site Constraint Screen

A bounded cross-utility diagnostic for data-center, AI-compute and industrial projects adding roughly 5–100 MW of load, where a late-discovered utility or permit constraint can strand schedule or capital.

The decision question

Before committing more land, design effort or capital: identify the most likely binding constraint across power, cooling, water and wastewater; estimate when it becomes binding; compare realistic sequencing or mitigation options; and state what evidence is still required before implementation.

  • Site / parcel and proposed load phasing
  • Known utility service, queue or interconnection context
  • Internal electrical equipment / usable-capacity basis
  • Cooling architecture and thermal assumptions
  • Water source, demand basis and allocation limits
  • Wastewater / pretreatment limits and discharge assumptions

Decision package

1. Constraint map and headroom table

2. Evidence register and assumption log

3. Base / alternative scenario comparison

4. CAPEX / OPEX and timing implications where supportable

5. What would change the conclusion

6. Ranked next actions and validation plan

Why the answer deserves confidence

Evidence-traced numbers, engine-controlled scenarios and explicit unknowns.

System & constraint map

Resource graph, capacity/load baseline, dependencies, measured versus theoretical limits, and the evidence supporting each material constraint.

Scenario economics

Low/base/high cases where uncertainty matters; operational impact, CAPEX/OPEX and timing with a calculation basis instead of a single unexplained number.

Decision confidence

Source-linked inputs, customer-stated inputs, modeled estimates, assumptions, unknowns and the exact evidence that would change the recommendation.

Prediction ledger

Material constraint predictions are structured so a later utility letter, peak test, permit or operating result can confirm or refute them.

Action register

Recommended action, owner, dependency, expected value, effort, evidence requirement, validation metric and sequence.

Verification handoff

Selected opportunities move through approval, implementation and verified-realized value without mixing forecast and actual outcome.
