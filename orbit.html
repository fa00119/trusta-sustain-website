R&D / commercialization

Infrastructure decisions across systems—not isolated dashboards.

ORBIT is Trusta's configurable infrastructure decision-intelligence engine for systems where energy, cooling, water, wastewater, utilities, equipment capacity, cost, resilience and operational constraints interact.

Commercial pathsPlatform architecture
Decision loop

Model → constrain → simulate → prioritize → explain.

The engine is designed to reason across interacting physical resources and preserve evidence confidence. It is not positioned as a universal autonomous control system or a substitute for required professional engineering review.

Common core

One engine, configurable domain modules.

ORBIT avoids rebuilding separate software for every sector. The common core handles system mapping, constraint reasoning, scenario analysis, economics and evidence confidence; domain modules supply sector-specific parameters and rules.

Infrastructure system map

Structured and semi-structured operating/design data are normalized into resources, capacities, loads, dependencies and evidence confidence.

Constraint + scenario intelligence

Identify binding constraints, cross-system dependencies, headroom and scenario exposure rather than optimizing one subsystem in isolation.

Decision economics

Estimate capacity recovery, operating-cost impact, implementation cost, payback, ROI and evidence required to improve confidence.

Campuses & large facilities

ORBIT answer contract

Every material recommendation must answer seven questions.

1–2

What constraint/opportunity exists—and why does it matter?

3–4

What action should be taken—and what happens under alternatives?

5–7

What is the operational/financial impact, confidence, and missing evidence?

Commercialization built in

Start bounded. Expand only when the evidence supports it.

Rapid screening

Structured infrastructure intake, constraint map, top opportunities, uncertainty and recommended evidence plan.

Paid diagnostic / calibrated pilot

Use real operating data to test one bounded technical/economic hypothesis and measure time-to-value.

Recurring / enterprise

Decision-support subscription, SaaS/API/integration deployment, or value-linked structures only where measurement and attribution are defensible.
