Why this matters

ORBIT should not start every site from a blank spreadsheet.

Public facts are not the same as customer-specific design inputs. Atlas separates them, timestamps them and carries their evidence state forward so a site screen begins with reusable prior knowledge rather than repeated manual discovery.

Source-linked prior knowledge

Water-management status, wastewater capacity, industrial pretreatment thresholds, utility reservations, permitting posture and infrastructure dependencies are normalized into one schema.

Freshness-aware

Draft, proposed, historical and current records remain explicitly labeled. A later source supersedes the earlier record instead of silently rewriting history.

Wired to predictions

Atlas records can serve as evidence or priors for ORBIT predictions, then be confirmed or contradicted by later site-specific or public evidence.
