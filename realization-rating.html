How to read the ratio

Divide total reported savings by the corresponding positive estimate or guarantee for the same cohort and metric. A result above 100% means reported savings exceeded that comparison basis. A contractual guarantee and a forecast answer different questions. The ratio of totals gives larger projects more weight.

The FY 2022 report prints 122.1% for reported versus guaranteed cost savings in §3.2. Its published dollar totals divide to 112.1%, which is the value shown here. Cost savings can include utility and operations savings. Measurement methods and adjustments vary across projects.

These rows are public program benchmarks. Trusta has not independently audited the underlying projects. The data do not identify individual vendor performance or support a vendor leaderboard.
