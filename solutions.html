Quick-start services

Focused work with a defined output.

Choose a starting point below or bring us a problem that does not fit neatly into a category.

01

AI Workflow Audit

Map a repetitive workflow, identify automation and decision-support opportunities, define data needs, risks, and a practical implementation path.

Typical output: workflow map + prioritized AI/automation roadmap.

02

Data & Dashboard Sprint

Turn fragmented operational, business, engineering, or compliance data into a decision-ready analysis, KPI model, dashboard concept, or reporting workflow.

Typical output: analysis + decision brief + dashboard specification.

03

Opportunity & Funding Scan

Identify and rank commercial, procurement, grant, SBIR/STTR, teaming, and partnership opportunities against your real capabilities and constraints.

Typical output: ranked shortlist + fit, blockers, evidence, and next actions.

04

RFP / NOFO Readiness Review

Convert a dense solicitation, RFP, regulation, or technical requirement set into a compliance matrix, evidence gaps, risks, and action plan.

Typical output: requirements matrix + go/no-go brief.

05

Environmental & Permit Gap Check

Review an industrial or development activity for likely environmental, wastewater, water, EHS, reporting, and permitting gaps before they become schedule problems.

Typical output: gap register + agency/action roadmap.

06

Water / Wastewater Diagnostic

Evaluate flows, sources, discharge pathways, monitoring needs, reuse opportunities, treatment constraints, and data gaps for industrial facilities.

Typical output: water balance + issue/opportunity map.

07

Data-Center Thermal & Cooling Review

Assess cooling, thermal headroom, water-energy interactions, capacity constraints, telemetry readiness, and opportunities for analytics or optimization.

Typical output: constraint map + pilot/optimization plan.

08

Energy & Infrastructure Capacity Review

Analyze facility capacity, utilities, cooling, water, energy interactions, operating constraints, and expansion scenarios.

Typical output: capacity model + prioritized interventions.

09

EHS System Improvement Sprint

Improve a compliance workflow, inspection program, training system, KPI process, audit trail, or operational control using better process design and automation.

Typical output: redesigned process + implementation package.

10

Sustainability & Carbon Data Readiness

Structure energy, emissions, water, waste, supplier, and operational data for reporting, target-setting, assurance, or internal decision support.

Typical output: data model + gap assessment + reporting roadmap.

11

Technical Feasibility / Due Diligence

Independent technical review of an engineering concept, technology, vendor proposal, investment thesis, infrastructure plan, or proposed project.

Typical output: evidence-based technical decision memo.

12

Prototype & Applied R&D Sprint

Turn a hard technical or operational problem into a testable concept, model, proof of concept, experiment plan, or prototype architecture.

Typical output: prototype concept + validation plan + scale path.
