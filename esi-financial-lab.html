Explicit model equations

payable_before = max(0, baseline × covered_fraction − allowances)

payable_after = max(0, max(0,baseline − reduction) × covered_fraction − allowances)

modeled_value = (payable_before − payable_after) × assumed_price

net_project_cost = max(0, capital_cost − hypothetical_incentive)

This simplified model intentionally excludes transaction costs, time value of money, baseline drift, allocation rules, jurisdiction-specific compliance obligations, leakage, rebound, tax treatment and verification. Free allowances, carbon taxes, offset credits and CBAM certificates are not interchangeable legal instruments.
