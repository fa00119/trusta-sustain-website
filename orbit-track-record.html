Verification contract

Prediction → timestamp → evidence candidate → human review → resolution → calibration.

The public methodology is intentionally simple. Patent-sensitive model mechanics are not disclosed here.

1 · Register

A released screen emits a falsifiable claim, probability, evidence reference, named resolution source and due date.

2 · Observe

Official source changes can generate immutable resolution candidates. A candidate is evidence, not a verdict.

3 · Resolve

Final public outcomes require a separate human review and are appended as confirmed, refuted, partial or void.

4 · Calibrate

Accepted non-void outcomes update public Brier score and directional hit rate. Original forecasts remain unchanged.

Public desk-screen probability bands: 3/3 scenario agreement = 0.85; 2/3 = 0.65; 1/3 = 0.35; 0/3 = 0.10. Lower-confidence claims are retained so the public record does not select only easy forecasts.

Privacy & integrity

Customer predictions are private by default.

Private by default

Customer site predictions are not published without authorization. Public calibration uses only predictions explicitly marked public.

No silent edits

Forecasts, evidence candidates, reviews and resolutions follow append-only records. A correction does not overwrite history.

Bounded public output

The public feed exposes accepted outcomes and aggregate calibration only. Reviewer identity, candidate packets, private predictions and internal rationale remain non-public.
