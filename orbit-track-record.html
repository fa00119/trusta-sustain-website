Verification contract

Prediction → timestamp → resolution → calibration.

The public methodology is intentionally simple. Patent-sensitive model mechanics are not disclosed here.

1 · Register

A released screen emits a falsifiable claim, probability, evidence reference, named resolution source and due date.

2 · Chain

The record is canonicalized, SHA-256 hashed and linked to the previous ledger hash. Corrections create superseding records; history is not overwritten.

3 · Resolve

An official permit action, utility docket update, project filing or other named source records confirmed, refuted, partial or void.

4 · Calibrate

As outcomes accumulate, ORBIT reports measures such as Brier score and directional hit rate by constraint type.

Public desk-screen probability bands for this launch batch: 3/3 scenario agreement = 0.85; 2/3 = 0.65; 1/3 = 0.35; 0/3 = 0.10.

Privacy & integrity

Customer predictions are private by default.

Private by default

Customer site predictions are not published without authorization. Customers retain their own prediction and verification history inside the ORBIT workspace.

No silent edits

Core forecast and resolution records are append-only. A correction creates a new record pointing to the superseded prediction.

Public anchoring

The public registry and chain head are committed to a public Git history so later backfilling is detectable.
