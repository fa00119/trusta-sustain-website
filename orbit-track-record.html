Why zero is intentional

The clock starts now. History is not manufactured.

Trusta is publishing the registry before it has a favorable track record to advertise. Public counts remain zero until a prediction is actually registered. Resolved counts remain zero until the named external evidence arrives. This is deliberate: the value of the record comes from making predictions before outcomes are known.

Verification contract

Prediction → timestamp → resolution → calibration.

The public methodology is intentionally simple. Patent-sensitive model mechanics are not disclosed here.

1 · Register

A released screen emits a falsifiable claim, probability, evidence IDs, phase/date window, named resolution-source type and due date.

2 · Chain

The record is canonicalized, SHA-256 hashed and linked to the previous ledger hash. Corrections create superseding records; history is not overwritten.

3 · Resolve

A utility letter, permit action, peak test, operating measurement or other named source records confirmed, refuted, partial or void.

4 · Calibrate

As outcomes accumulate, ORBIT reports calibration measures such as Brier score and directional hit rate by constraint type.

Privacy & integrity

Customer predictions are private by default.

Private by default

Customer site predictions are not published without authorization. Customers can retain their own prediction and verification history inside the ORBIT workspace.

No silent edits

Core forecast and resolution records are append-only. A correction creates a new record that points to the superseded prediction.

External anchoring

Ledger chain-head hashes are designed to be periodically anchored to an independent public timestamp so later backfilling can be detected.

The compounding asset

Analysis ends. Evidence accumulates.

The long-term ORBIT asset is not a report archive. It is a growing record of what was predicted, what evidence supported it, what happened, and how the model should change next.
