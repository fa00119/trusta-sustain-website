Trusta Decision Standard

Managed reports go beyond a score and recommendation list.

The exact depth varies by purchased scope, but material managed assessments are designed to preserve the evidence, logic, uncertainty and implementation path behind the conclusion.

Executive decision summary

Decision question, baseline/current state, strongest findings, material risks, confidence and recommended next move.

Evidence + scoring map

Evidence register, scoring logic, material assumptions, source/customer/modeled states, gaps and unresolved unknowns.

Risk + impact register

Prioritized gaps and risks plus economic or operational impact where the supplied evidence supports quantification.

Implementation sequence

Priority actions, dependencies, owners or owner roles, sequencing and practical decision gates.

Verification plan

What should be measured next, which evidence closes uncertainty, and what would confirm or overturn the recommendation.

Review mark where earned

TRUSTA REVIEWED / Trusta Decision Standard is applied only when the applicable review gate was actually completed; it is not a regulatory or professional certification.
