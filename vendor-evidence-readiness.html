Six checks before presenting results

01 · Define the claim

Specify the metric, equipment/system boundary, time interval and exactly what the buyer should infer. Avoid implying a general warranty from one narrow observation.

02 · Identify source provenance

Record who generated each measurement or artifact, when it was collected, whether access/usage rights are valid and whether the original evidence can be reproduced.

03 · Document calibration

State instrument calibration status, sensor accuracy, units, missing time periods and any preprocessing that may affect conclusions.

04 · Separate data from models

Observed site outcomes, synthetic tests, replay results, forecasts and simulated scenarios are distinct evidence categories. Label each accordingly.

05 · Explain uncertainty

Specify the baseline, assumptions, exclusions, potential confounders and any sensitivity analysis. Flag disagreement rather than forcing a PASS.

06 · Make review repeatable

Preserve an authorized trace from claim to source records, applicable processing versions, reviewer notes and unresolved exceptions.
