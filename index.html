The ORBIT decision

Which resource blocks the site, when, and what should happen next?

ORBIT treats large-load infrastructure as one connected decision problem instead of separate utility, cooling, water and permit studies.

01

Predict

Map the site, load phases, utility context and cross-resource constraints to identify where headroom disappears.

02

Decide

Compare realistic sequencing and mitigation options with transparent assumptions, uncertainty and economics where supportable.

03

Verify & learn

Track whether the predicted constraint and expected value actually materialize. Confirmed outcomes become calibration evidence for future decisions.

Public proof

A real project analyzed from official public evidence.

Our Memphis public case demonstrates the method without implying a customer relationship or inventing private inputs. It separates what official records support from what must remain unknown.

Power dependency

Staged utility service, transmission, substation and approval milestones are treated as dated constraints—not as proof of usable IT capacity.

Water evolution

Different official planning capacities are preserved by source date instead of silently reconciled to a convenient answer.

Regulatory gate

Physical reuse capacity and operating authorization remain separate evidence gates.

Why the numbers hold up

Every material conclusion is tied to a source, a customer-stated input, a modeled estimate, an assumption or an explicit unknown. Invariant QA checks the decision logic rather than merely repeating an expected answer.
