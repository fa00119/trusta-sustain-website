ORBIT · Infrastructure Decisions

Every decision can become calibration evidence.

Most infrastructure analysis ends with a report. ORBIT is designed so the decision leaves behind a dated, testable prediction and the evidence required to resolve it later.

01

Predict

Map load phases and cross-resource constraints, then register what is expected to bind, when, with explicit probability and evidence.

02

Resolve

Utility letters, permits, peak tests and operating evidence later confirm, refute or partially confirm the prediction.

03

Learn

Resolved outcomes become calibration data by constraint type, region, phase and engine version.
