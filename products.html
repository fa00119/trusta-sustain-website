Revenue OS

Operating core

Revenue OS is Trusta's internal execution engine for opportunity intake, scoring, evidence/provenance discipline, human approval of restricted actions, customer/order workflows, and revenue operations. It is not marketed as a separate downloadable product today; it is the operating core from which customer-facing opportunity and revenue workflows are productized.

Find & qualify

Normalize opportunities and prospects, separate real from synthetic/test evidence, and rank fit without treating every lead as pipeline.

Preserve evidence

Keep source, confidence, blockers, assumptions, and unknowns attached to decisions.

Control actions

Use explicit human gates around outreach, submissions, spending, and other consequential external actions.

Technical / enterprise intelligence

Infrastructure Intelligence

R&D / commercialization track

A technical decision-support direction for data-center and industrial systems where compute, power, cooling, water, thermal capacity, operations, and cost interact.

Why it exists

AI-density growth makes isolated power, thermal, water, and workload decisions increasingly expensive. The engine direction is to model those interactions together rather than optimize one subsystem blindly.

Where it is now

Trusta has R&D architecture, technical concepts, prototype/analysis work, and commercialization outreach. This is a high-value enterprise wedge, not currently represented as a one-click public SaaS purchase.

How customers engage

Only bounded technical assessments, pilots, or custom enterprise projects should be sold until the engine reaches a standardized production interface and validated customer outcomes.

How the pieces compound

Software value comes from reuse—not from calling every service an app.

A requirement parser, scoring model, provenance layer, secure delivery workflow, customer identity, billing, and certificate/verification system can support many products. Reuse lowers marginal delivery cost and makes recurring software revenue possible, but product value ultimately depends on customers, usage, retention, revenue, and defensibility.

Shared core

Identity, secure customer access, billing, entitlements, artifact delivery, evidence/provenance, and decision structures.

Vertical products

Opportunity, proposal/evidence, AI governance, data quality, infrastructure, and other workflows can sit on the common core.

Commercial proof

The next valuation step is not another product name—it is paid use, repeat usage, recurring revenue, conversion, and measurable customer outcomes.
