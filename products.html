First commercial vertical

Non-dilutive R&D funding intelligence for technical small businesses.

The platform remains extensible to other opportunity classes, but the first focused workflow is federal and non-dilutive R&D funding where eligibility, registrations, evidence, deadlines and review criteria create recurring decision friction.

The job to be done

Turn a stream of solicitations into a disciplined pursue / watch / dismiss decision with enough evidence to know what to do next—without rebuilding the company's profile and readiness state for every opportunity.

Fit & readiness: technical fit, company eligibility, timing, evidence gaps and blockers.Go / no-go memory: why an opportunity was pursued, watched or dismissed and what changed later.Requirement calendar: deadline, registrations, attachments, letters and other pursuit prerequisites.Review-criteria reasoning: evaluate a pursuit against the agency's published criteria where available.

Current foundation vs expansion

Available foundation: organization identity/context, opportunity reasoning, saved decision state, billing/entitlements, secure customer/order flows and evidence-aware logic.

Expanding: reusable evidence vault, richer registration-readiness tracking, source coverage, requirement calendars, team workflows and expert red-team upgrade paths.

Items labeled “expanding” are roadmap direction, not claims that every feature is currently live.

Why not just use a chatbot?

The product should compound decision memory, not merely generate text.

Persistent organizational context

Capabilities, constraints, registrations, recurring evidence and prior decisions can stay attached to the workspace rather than being reconstructed from prompts.

Versioned decision state

Fit, readiness, blockers, unknowns, pursuit status and next actions form an operating record across multiple opportunity cycles.

Human upgrade when warranted

A high-value pursuit can move into a bounded expert red-team review without turning the entire software product into a consulting business.

Shared trust layer

Evidence Intelligence is infrastructure underneath the products.

It remains part of Trusta's proprietary engine architecture, but buyers should experience its value through traceable outputs rather than being asked to buy another abstract label.

Evidence → interpretation → assumption/model → recommendation → validation → outcome

Source-linked facts, customer-stated information, modeled estimates, assumptions, hypotheses and unknowns remain distinct. Material outputs can show what evidence supports a conclusion and what new evidence would change it.
