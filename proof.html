Standard Assessment

A real decision package has traceability.

A managed assessment is structured to show the basis for each material finding rather than presenting unexplained scores.

Executive layer

  • decision summary and explicit go / narrow / stop posture
  • scope and decision question
  • dimension scores with stated rubric and weights
  • material risks, controls and escalation gates
  • implementation sequence and KPI plan

Evidence layer

  • evidence IDs and evidence states
  • customer-stated inputs kept separate from source-linked facts
  • modeled assumptions and confidence labels
  • what-would-change-the-conclusion tests
  • evidence-to-finding traceability and review record

ORBIT Large-Load Site Constraint Screen

Which constraint binds first, when, and what is the lowest-regret sequence around it?

The illustrative screen connects power, cooling, water, wastewater, capacity, schedule, economics, uncertainty and reversibility rather than treating each utility as an isolated study.

Constraint package

  • phased load baseline and capacity/headroom map
  • power/interconnection timing screen
  • cooling plant vs distribution constraint screen
  • water and wastewater evidence posture
  • cross-resource interaction map
  • scenario comparison and sensitivity

Decision package

  • identified vs modeled value kept explicit
  • assumption and risk registers
  • 90-day evidence acquisition plan

  • licensed-engineering boundary

Quality gate

Release requires more than a polished PDF.

G0 Intake

Scope, evidence, decision question and owner are usable.

G1 Evidence

Material claims have basis; unknowns remain visible.

G2 Logic QA

Scores, calculations and recommendations tie to the stated method.

G3/G4 Red-team & release

Contrary interpretations, limitations, confidence and reviewer record are present.
