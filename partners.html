Four ways to work together

Referral / Introduction

For eligible independently introduced commercial customers: proposed 10–15% of the first engagement's net cash actually collected. Subject to written agreement and eligibility review; no commission is earned or payable merely by submitting an introduction.

Reseller & White-label

Use agreed wholesale economics or customer-facing co-branding. Delivery ownership, quality checks, IP, licensing, liability and support responsibilities must be documented before offers.

Technical Co-delivery

Energy consultants, engineers, software vendors and equipment suppliers can combine their field expertise with Trusta's bounded decision and evidence workflows.

Civilian Subcontracting

We evaluate non-defense, non-security public/private technical scopes such as benchmarking support, data quality, technical diligence and engineering analytics; only where qualified and authorized.

Referral safeguards: Proposed commission applies only to a qualifying new introduction accepted in writing, excludes existing clients and previously active opportunities, and is calculated on eligible net service fees actually received after discounts, refunds and applicable taxes. Any reversal or nonpayment requires contractual reconciliation. No government procurement kickbacks, regulated or undisclosed conflicts of interest, or compensation prohibited by law or customer policy. No commission, percentage, exclusivity or partnership is binding before mutually signed terms.
