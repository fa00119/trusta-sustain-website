What the pilot delivers

Set the baseline

Agree on the site boundary, time period, production measure, and relevant utility charges. Flag gaps in available records.

Explain cost drivers

Compare usage and peak demand with production and schedules. Show which observations can be supported by the available data.

Prioritize next steps

Deliver a baseline workbook, a short decision brief, and scenarios with their assumptions so your team can decide what to investigate or test.
