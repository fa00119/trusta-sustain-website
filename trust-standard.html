TRUSTA / TRUST STANDARD
Evidence · Method · QA

Trust should come from traceability—not from confident wording.

Trusta separates source evidence, customer statements, modeled values, assumptions, hypotheses and unknowns. Paid deliverables are designed to expose the reasoning and uncertainty behind a recommendation and to pass a defined quality gate before client release.

Review marks

Three labels with intentionally different meanings.

TRUSTA REVIEWED

Artifact QA completed

Used when the paid artifact passes the applicable content, factual-consistency, arithmetic/table, scope and unsupported-claim review.

TRUSTA VERIFIED METHOD

Versioned method applied

Used when a defined Trusta method or scoring framework was followed and the required method checks passed. It does not mean every customer-provided fact was independently verified.

EVIDENCE VERIFIED

Item-level source support

Used only for a fact or value directly supported by reviewed source evidence. It is not applied globally to estimates, customer statements or modeled outputs.

Release gate

What a managed deliverable must survive before release.

Evidence check

Can material claims be traced to their basis?

Logic check

Do scores/calculations/recommendations follow the stated method?

Scope check

Did the artifact answer the purchased question without pretending to cover more?

Claim check

Are unsupported certainty, guarantees and misleading labels removed?

Important boundary

A Trusta mark describes process quality, not regulatory authority.

Unless separately and explicitly established, Trusta review marks are not legal certification, government approval, audit assurance, accredited education, professional engineering stamping, cybersecurity certification or a guarantee of savings, revenue, funding, eligibility or compliance.
