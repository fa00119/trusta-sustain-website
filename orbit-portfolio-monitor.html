System of record behavior

Changes are events. Decisions become versions. Neither is silently rewritten.

ORBIT's monitoring architecture is designed to preserve an auditable history. A source change creates a new event. The resulting alert is append-only. Human review is recorded separately. If the recommendation changes, Decision Memory creates a new version linked to the evidence that triggered review while the prior version remains inspectable.

Portfolio-scoped

One customer can maintain multiple sites and multiple monitored decision scopes under one portfolio.

Evidence-linked

Alerts inherit the official source and affected Atlas or Prediction Ledger references so the reason for a re-screen remains inspectable.

Versioned decisions

Decision Memory preserves the recommendation, rationale, effective date, linked alerts and evidence snapshot of each version.

Annual ORBIT

From one-time screen to recurring decision intelligence.

The commercial path is designed around a bounded diagnostic first, then recurring monitoring only where the customer has a live reason to keep the decision current.

1 · Initial screen

Establish the cross-resource baseline, evidence register, constraint map and testable predictions.

2 · Monitoring scope

Select the sites, jurisdictions, evidence domains and open predictions whose changes can affect the decision.

3 · Change-driven decision versioning

When material evidence changes, review the signal, determine whether the decision basis moved, and preserve the new recommendation as another version rather than rewriting the prior record.
