Trusta Delivery Standard

Small offer does not mean low-quality work.

Every paid Trusta service is structured to produce a usable decision, workflow, artifact, dataset, report, or next-step plan—not a generic slide deck.

1. Scope & inputs

We clarify the question, boundaries, required inputs, assumptions, and success criteria before substantive work begins.

2. Finished deliverables

Outputs are defined up front and may include editable files, spreadsheets, memos, reports, matrices, dashboards, templates, workflows, or other agreed formats.

3. QA & evidence posture

Material assumptions, unknowns, limitations, source/evidence posture, formula or logic checks, and unresolved risks are made explicit when relevant.

4. Handoff

Every service ends with a concise handoff that explains what was delivered, how to use it, open items, and recommended next actions.

5. Reusable where practical

Whenever useful, Trusta provides editable or reusable versions so the customer is not locked into a static one-time artifact.

6. Completion evidence

Eligible live training can include a dated Trusta Certificate of Completion tied to the participant, course/session, and recorded completion requirements.
