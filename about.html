Trusta Sustain Corporation

Infrastructure decisions should get smarter every time they are tested against reality.

Trusta Sustain Corporation is a Missouri C-Corporation headquartered in Los Angeles. The company is building ORBIT, infrastructure decision intelligence for large-load systems where power, onsite electrical capacity, cooling, water, wastewater, schedule and economics interact.

Founder

Farhad Azarmi, Ph.D.

Founder & CEO

Farhad Azarmi is an environmental and infrastructure engineer with a Ph.D. in Civil & Environmental Engineering from the University of Surrey. His work spans industrial environmental systems, water and wastewater, utility constraints, sustainability, data-center infrastructure, technical decision support and applied R&D.

Trusta is being built around a specific founder-market fit: complex infrastructure decisions often fail at the seams between disciplines. ORBIT is designed to connect those seams into one evidence-controlled decision model.

Founder expertise is a strength today and a concentration risk the company intends to reduce through specialist hires, design partners and channel relationships as the commercial base expands.

Company thesis

Predict → Decide → Implement → Verify → Learn.

The long-term product is not a collection of reports. It is a decision-intelligence system that records what was predicted, what was decided, what happened and what should improve next time.

01

ORBIT

Predict where infrastructure headroom disappears, compare sequencing options and identify the evidence required before implementation.

02

Verification

Carry selected recommendations through approval, implementation and measured outcome without mixing forecast and realized value.

03

Learning layer

Confirmed and refuted predictions can become calibration evidence for future sites, regions and portfolios.

What is commercial now

One core venture story.

ORBIT is the primary commercial and fundraising story. YIELD functions as its value-verification layer. Evidence Intelligence is the trust architecture underneath both.

What remains broader

R&D and secondary products stay clearly separated.

Opportunity Intelligence, training, assessments, Method Packs and broader research programs remain available or under development, but they are intentionally secondary to the infrastructure-intelligence thesis.

Federal readiness

Trusta is an active SAM-registered U.S. contractor. Federal R&D and non-dilutive funding are used as technical-validation and commercialization tools, not substitutes for customer traction.

IP discipline

Trusta maintains a growing patent portfolio. Public materials describe the commercial problem and product behavior without disclosing patent-sensitive mechanisms.

Professional boundaries

Decision support does not replace licensed engineering, permit determinations, safety review or stamped design where those are required.
