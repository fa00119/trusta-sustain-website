Qualification

Good fit when the decision is bounded enough to analyze responsibly.

No sales call is required to determine basic fit. If the project falls outside the fixed scope, Trusta will not force it through a standard checkout.

Usually a fixed-scope fit

  • One site / parcel / facility or one clearly bounded expansion
  • One decision horizon and one primary decision question
  • Approx. 5–100 MW proposed or incremental load, or comparable industrial utility intensity
  • Existing evidence can be supplied for relevant utilities and onsite systems
  • No engineering stamp, permit approval, utility commitment or legal opinion is being purchased
  • The objective is constraint ranking, sequencing, evidence gaps and decision support

Needs custom written scope instead

  • Multi-site portfolio optimization as the initial scope
  • Detailed design, sealed drawings, construction documents or utility interconnection application
  • Regulated certification, statutory compliance determination or formal M&V assurance
  • Highly sensitive / export-controlled / classified information through public channels
  • Missing basic site/load evidence such that the task is primarily original field investigation
  • Production software integration, continuous monitoring or enterprise identity/security deployment

Constraint map

Cross-utility capacity/load baseline, dependencies, likely binding constraints and usable headroom where evidence supports it.

Evidence + assumptions

Source register, customer-stated inputs, modeled values, unknowns, comparison-basis checks and what would change the conclusion.

Decision package

Base/alternative scenario comparison, timing implications, CAPEX/OPEX where supportable, ranked actions and next validation steps.

Typical required inputs

  • Site / parcel and proposed load phasing
  • Utility service / queue / interconnection information
  • Onsite electrical one-lines or equipment capacity basis
  • Cooling architecture and thermal assumptions
  • Water source, demand basis and allocation limits where material
  • Wastewater / pretreatment / discharge basis where material
  • Known schedule commitments and decision deadline
  • Any current alternatives already under consideration
