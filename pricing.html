How value compounds

Pilot revenue is useful. Recurring intelligence is the business model.

The goal is not to sell repeated bespoke studies. ORBIT becomes more valuable when a customer keeps site evidence, decisions, predictions, source changes and verified outcomes in one governed workspace.

Annual monitoring
