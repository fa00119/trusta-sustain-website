What the credential means

A Trusta Certificate of Completion confirms that the named learner met the published requirements for the identified Trusta program and version. It can be verified online against Trusta's credential record.

What it does not mean

It is not an academic degree, professional license, government credential, accredited CEU, immigration credential, or independent industry certification unless a specific program separately states and substantiates such recognition.
