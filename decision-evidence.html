How commercial traction is measured

These are measurement definitions. Current figures are not disclosed on this page.

Scan-to-paid-pilot conversion
Consenting organizations that started a paid pilot within 90 days of their first completed scan, divided by identified scan organizations with a full 90-day observation window. Anonymous scans are counted separately.
Repeat purchase
Customers that bought a second engagement within 12 months of their first paid engagement, divided by first-time customers with a full 12-month observation window.
Monthly recurring revenue
Active contracted recurring service and software fees normalized to a monthly amount, excluding one-time engagements.
Decisions with measurable outcomes
Distinct decisions with a recorded forecast, documented baseline, and reviewed outcome on a comparable basis.
