What we analyze

Requirements first. Evidence second. Drafting third.

A strong response starts by understanding what must be proven, where the evidence lives, and which gaps can still be closed.

Requirement extraction

Identify mandatory requirements, evaluation criteria, instructions, forms, deliverables, deadlines, dependencies, and hidden compliance risks.

Compliance & evidence mapping

Map each requirement to available evidence, responsible owner, source document, status, gap, and next action.

Pursuit and response strategy

Clarify bid/no-bid factors, differentiators, proof points, response architecture, reviewer logic, and the work that must happen before submission.

Typical outputs

A decision-ready pursuit package.

Compliance matrix

Requirement-by-requirement matrix with evidence, ownership, status, risk, and response location.

Evidence-gap register

Missing proof, weak claims, unresolved questions, partner needs, certifications, attachments, and validation actions.

Response accelerator

Section structure, drafting prompts, approved source material, reviewer checklist, and controlled AI-assisted drafting workflow where appropriate.

Grant and R&D applications

NOFO requirement extraction, eligibility logic, evidence mapping, partner requirements, work-plan structure, and submission readiness.

Enterprise proposals

Customer requirements, technical proposals, implementation scopes, due-diligence responses, and complex commercial questionnaires.
